# Virtual Office — Canonical Floor Plan

## Spatial grammar

The office is one single-level campus for 6–14 authored agents. A curved circulation loop gently branches around a planted central hub. It must read as a workplace from the overview even when all labels are hidden.

```text
                         north / daylight glazing

        Strategy cluster                 Creative cluster
      B02 — pillar research             D01 — copy studio
      B03 — publishing plan              D02 — visual studio
              \                                  /
               \      A01 coordination          /
                \           hub                 /
                 └──── central tree / social ──┘
                                 |
                           E01 quality gate

                  south / quiet expansion reserve
```

## Cluster intent

| Zone | Agents | Visual signature | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Coordination | A01, upper-central | calm command desk, planning-board abstraction | Workflow handoff and orientation |
| Strategy | B02, B03, west wing | research shelf / calendar materials | Early-stage thinking and planning |
| Creative | D01, D02, east wing | writing desk / pin-up or media review surface | Content and visual production |
| Quality | E01, downstream south | review table, comparison surface | Final quality gate |
| Social hub | none owned | sculptural tree, seating, coffee point | Ambient pause only; never task semantics |

## Ownership and reserve

Each workstation has fixed geometry, prop language and location. New real agents add a deliberately designed pod; they never procedurally spawn a desk. Leave visually integrated reserve around the south/east edge without labels such as “Coming soon”.

## Camera

Default: mild-perspective 3/4 overview, enough height to read all six workstations. Guided focus may move closer to the selected agent but preserves a visible spatial relationship to the hub. On narrow mobile screens, preserve selection connection but put the popup in a safe floating screen position.
