## Active scene architecture

`VirtualOffice` owns the DOM shell, HUD, popup and accessible roster. `OfficeCanvas` owns the WebGL boundary, guided overview/focus camera and constrained orbit controls. `GardenOfficeScene` owns active light/fog/contact shadow setup. `GardenOfficeModel` loads one baked environment GLB, adds six state-connected hotspots/labels, and mounts six separate character GLBs through `RiggedAgentCharacter`.

The active environment is `garden-office-v7.glb`. `OfficeLighting.tsx`, `OfficeRoom.tsx`, `WorkstationDesk.tsx`, the legacy primitive `AgentCharacter`, CEO controller, celebration effect and handoff effect are not part of the active garden scene. They are legacy/alternative seams and should not be mixed back into the rebuild.

## Visual gap against benchmark

- Composition: current view has a flat single-level floor and weak edge framing; benchmark has curved level changes and deliberate foreground/midground/background.
- Architecture: the rear waterfall and Focus Zone read as props rather than occupied architectural volumes.
- Tree: correct focal position, but trunk/crown silhouette is coarse and the planter feels detached from circulation.
- Stations: correct six-agent radial logic, but repeated small desks, black monitor slabs and limited role props make the scene read as a prototype.
- Characters: true 3D but small, rigid and materially simple; pose integration is improved yet not production character quality.
- Materials: stone dominates value and scale; wood, fabric, glass and water do not separate strongly enough at overview distance.
- Light: browser lighting is brighter than earlier versions, but the scene lacks reflection fill and a controlled bright/shade hierarchy.
- Life: screens and joint motion exist, but foliage/water/environmental motion is minimal.

## Refactor / rebuild targets

1. Create a new versioned Blender wrapper rather than continuing v7 micro-patches.
2. Replace repeated round pots with fewer integrated organic beds and layered circulation.
3. Turn the left rear feature and right Focus Zone into two visually occupied pavilions.
4. Add role props and silhouette variation at the six stations while preserving exact hotspot coordinates.
5. Add a local procedural environment map/lightformer setup and keep one shadow-casting sun.
6. Retune runtime material handling, label scale and screen/water motion in the active model only.
7. Keep the current state adapter, selection, popup, CTA and accessibility paths unchanged.
