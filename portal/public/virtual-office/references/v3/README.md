# Virtual Office v3 art-direction pack

These images are production references for the authored Blender scene. They are not rendered as a flat office background.

## Locked visual system

- Camera: wide 16:9, elevated three-quarter architectural view, approximately a 28–35 mm visual feel.
- Hierarchy: limestone plaza first, ficus second, six oak workstations third, water and tropical planting as framing layers.
- Palette: warm limestone, medium oak, champagne bronze, graphite upholstery, low-iron glass, cyan display emission, deep foliage green.
- Light: late-morning sun, crisp leaf breakup, warm practical lanterns, subtle water caustic color; no neon wash.
- Detail priority: tree silhouette and roots, workstation furniture, pavilion interior, paving/water boundary, then people at overview scale.

## Final generation prompt set

All generations used the supplied office visual and/or a previously locked reference image strictly as style and proportion references.

1. Overview: photoreal tropical office atrium without UI, preserving six circular stations, monumental ficus, perimeter water, waterfall wall, Focus Pavilion, warm limestone/oak/bronze, and a wide elevated architectural camera.
2. Workstation: one dimensionally consistent crescent workstation shown in four large studio views, with oak top, fluted stone cabinetry, bronze trim, graphite chair, three transparent cyan displays, articulated arms, and realistic desk props.
3. Ficus: one mature multi-trunk ficus in front, side, and top views plus large root, branch, and leaf details; botanically plausible and free of spherical topiary shapes.
4. Pavilions: isolated buildable views of the glass water-curtain wall and curved glass/bronze/wood focus lounge, including shelves, sofa, planters, seams, and practical lights.
5. Agents: six distinct Vietnamese creative-technology professionals in consistent seated office-chair poses, realistic anatomy, smart-casual wardrobe, and neutral studio light.
6. Materials: eight PBR validation groups for limestone, oak, fluted stone, bronze, clear glass, graphite fabric, cyan display glass, and shallow turquoise water.
7. Foliage atlas: sixteen isolated photoreal ficus twig/leaf clusters in a 4x4 grid on genuine transparency, with clean gutters and minimal baked shadow.
8. Limestone texture: seamless orthographic warm honed limestone albedo, flat cross-polarized lighting, no grout, cracks, objects, or baked highlights.
9. Oak texture: seamless orthographic quarter-sawn medium oak albedo, vertical restrained grain, flat cross-polarized lighting, no plank seams, objects, or baked highlights.
10. Ficus bark texture: seamless orthographic mature ficus bark albedo with gray-brown fused growth ridges, shallow fissures, restrained moss traces, and no baked lighting.

All prompts explicitly excluded text, logos, watermarks, cartoon geometry, and impossible floating parts.
