## Iteration gates

- Iteration A — composition: camera, room depth, adult scale, circulation and hero silhouettes only.
- Iteration B — assets/materials: workstation, agent, ficus, pavilion, waterfall, UV scale and PBR response.
- Iteration C — light/runtime: sun direction, exposure, glass/water readability, batching, texture size and browser integration.
