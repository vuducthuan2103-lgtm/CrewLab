# CrewLab Character Visual QA

Use the scorecard in `docs/virtual-office/CHARACTER_BIBLE.md`. A polished environment cannot compensate for a rejected character.

## Required evidence

- Neutral front, three-quarter, side, and rear turntable views
- Face close-up in neutral and expressive states
- Seated front and side views at the actual workstation
- Typing, reviewing/listening, success, waiting, and error frames
- Office-camera screenshot showing scale, contrast, and readability
- Asset metrics and licence register entry

## Review order

1. Silhouette and adult stylized proportions
2. Face topology, eyes, expression readability, and asymmetry
3. Hair mass, hairline, strand-card artifacts, and specular response
4. Hands, fingers, wrists, shoulders, and deformation
5. Clothing construction, layer separation, folds, seams, and clipping
6. Material consistency under the office lighting rig
7. Seated ergonomics and workstation contact
8. Role identity and differentiation from the other five agents
9. Readability at the production camera distance

## Decision

Score every Character Bible category and list concrete defects by severity. A01 passes only when every hard blocker is resolved and the total meets the documented threshold. If it fails, provide a bounded revision list and keep B02-E01 blocked. Do not soften the verdict because work has already been invested.
